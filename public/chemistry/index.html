Beaker:

Beakers are cylindrical in shape with a flat bottom. They are generally made of glass and are graduated with markings to measure liquids accurately. Most also have a small spout (or “beak”) to aid pouring. Beakers come in different sizes, with the most common being 50 ml, 100 ml, 250 ml, 500 ml etc.
