Communication Introduction

January 11, 2025

COMMUNICATION

Communication is the process of exchanging information, ideas, thoughts, emotions, and feelings through speech, signals, writing, or behavior.

TYPES OF COMMUNICATION

  • Verbal Communication
  • Non-Verbal Communication

LEVELS OF COMMUNICATION

  • Intrapersonal Communication:
    Communication that occurs within one’s own mind, influenced by personal feelings, biases, prejudices, and beliefs.

  • Interpersonal Communication:
    Interaction between two people, which can extend to informal conversations involving more participants.

  • One-to-Group Communication:
    Involves a speaker addressing an audience with the aim of informing or motivating them.

  • Mass Communication:
    Transmission of information to a large audience via electronic or written mediums.
    Examples: Television, channels, books, etc.

THE 7 C’s OF COMMUNICATION

  1. Candidness
  2. Clarity
  3. Completeness
  4. Conciseness
  5. Concreteness
  6. Correctness
  7. Courtesy

EXPLANATION OF THE 7 C’s

  • Candidness:
    Express your thoughts openly and honestly without hiding anything.

  • Clarity:
    Clearly define your idea and convey it in simple, understandable words.

  • Completeness:
    Provide the full message, leaving no room for assumptions or guesses by the receiver.

  • Conciseness:
    Deliver your message using as few words as possible while retaining its meaning.

  • Concreteness:
    Use specific facts, figures, and tangible details to support your message.

  • Correctness:
    Ensure the use of correct language, grammar, spelling, and accurate information.

  • Courtesy:
    Communicate with politeness and respect, choosing words thoughtfully.
